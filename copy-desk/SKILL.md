# COPY DESK — press content

## What this is

COPY DESK is the Press department's content instrument: the verified fact-blocks, bios, and copy an agent needs to write about CWI accurately — names, places, credits, and milestones sourced from the label, not from memory.

Department: **press** | Category: `press-content` | Slot: `utility` | Version: `1.0.0`

## When to use

- You are writing a bio, announcement, or article and need verified facts.
- You need to avoid the known errors (e.g. 'Ahkeem' vs 'King Akeem', recording-studio locations).
- You want machine-readable copy blocks to paste into posts.
