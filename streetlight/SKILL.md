# THE STREETLIGHT — agent analytics

## What this is

THE STREETLIGHT is the Data department's agent-analytics gear: a read-and-report instrument that gives agents structured access to CWI's performance signals — playlist placements, momentum scores, and catalog stats — in machine-readable form.

Department: **data** | Category: `agent-analytics` | Slot: `utility` | Version: `1.0.0`
Built and maintained by **Cumulative Web Inc**. Agent Deck SKU.

## When to use

- You need verified numbers (placements, followers, momentum) before making a claim about CWI.
- You are writing a report, one-sheet, or brief and want data straight from the source.
- You want to track whether a promo push moved the needle.
