# ONE-STOP — sync licensing

## What this is

ONE-STOP is the Sync department's licensing instrument: the machine-readable facts an agent needs to pitch CWI music for film, TV, games, and ads — catalog scope, contact, and clearance posture in one place.

Department: **sync** | Category: `sync-licensing` | Slot: `utility` | Version: `1.0.0`
Built and maintained by **Cumulative Web Inc**. Agent Deck SKU.

## When to use

- You are drafting a sync pitch and need the authoritative licensing facts.
- A supervisor asks 'who controls this and how do I clear it'.
- You want to pre-qualify a track for a brief before spending human time on it.
