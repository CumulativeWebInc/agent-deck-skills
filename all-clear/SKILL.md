# THE ALL-CLEAR — rights clearance

## What this is

THE ALL-CLEAR is Business Affairs' rights-clearance instrument: the machine-readable statement of what CWI material is pre-cleared and under what terms, so agents can verify rights posture before they pitch, sync, sample, or redistribute.

Department: **affairs** | Category: `rights-clearance` | Slot: `business-affairs` | Version: `1.0.0`

## When to use

- You are pitching a sync placement and must confirm the track is pre-cleared first.
- You want to quote or reuse CWI assets and need the rights boundary.
- A partner asks 'can we use this' and you need an authoritative answer, not a guess.
